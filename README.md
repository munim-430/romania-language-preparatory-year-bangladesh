## 🇪🇺 Why Romania & *Anul Pregătitor* for Bangladeshi Applicants?

Romania offers one of the most accessible and academically solid gateways into the European Union and European Higher Education Area (EHEA):

1. **Study Gap Flexibility**: Unlike several Western European nations that impose rigid restrictions on academic study gaps, Romanian public universities regularly accept applicants with **3 to 10+ year study gaps** for the Romanian preparatory language foundation year (*Anul Pregătitor*).
2. **No Mandatory English Language Test**: Admission to the preparatory year does not require IELTS/TOEFL scores. Students master the Romanian language for one academic year before transitioning into full-degree Bachelor's or Master's programs.
3. **Schengen Integration**: Romania is fully integrated into European frameworks, allowing enhanced educational mobility across the Schengen Zone.
4. **Direct University Remittance**: 100% of academic tuition is paid directly by the student via official Bangladeshi banking channels (SWIFT) directly into the state university's treasury or Romanian commercial bank account (BCR, BRD, Banca Transilvania). No intermediary handling of statutory fees is permitted.

## 📜 Key Document: Ministry Acceptance Letter (*Scrisoare de Acceptare la Studii*)

Unlike ordinary acceptance letters issued by admissions offices, Romanian university entry for international non-EU citizens requires an official **Acceptance Letter issued directly by the Ministry of National Education of Romania (Ministerul Educației)**.

### Acceptance Letter Workflow:
1. Student submits apostilled/authenticated academic documents to university admissions.
2. University evaluates eligibility and forwards the file to the Ministry in Bucharest.
3. The Ministry validates the credentials and issues the **Scrisoare de Acceptare la Studii**.
4. The student uses this official government document to initiate the direct university tuition bank wire and apply for the Type D/VS long-stay student visa.

## 📋 Comprehensive Checklist for Bangladeshi Applicants

### Stage 1: Document Authentication in Dhaka
- [x] **SSC & HSC / Bachelor's Certificates and Transcripts**:
  - Verification by respective Education Board / University.
  - Attestation by Education Ministry (Shikkha Bhaban, Dhaka).
  - Attestation by Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA, Segunbagicha, Dhaka).
  - Certified translation into Romanian or English with authorized notary seal.
- [x] **Birth Certificate**: MOFA-attested and translated.
- [x] **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)**: Issued by Bangladesh Police and verified by MOFA.

### Stage 2: Direct Tuition Bank Wire via Student File
- [x] Upon receipt of the Ministry Acceptance Letter, the applicant visits an authorized Bangladeshi foreign exchange bank (e.g., Eastern Bank, City Bank, Prime Bank).
- [x] Open an official student file.
- [x] Execute direct outward SWIFT remittance of the tuition fee directly to the university's official Romanian IBAN.
- [x] Collect official SWIFT MT103 confirmation receipt.

### Stage 3: Consular Visa Application (Embassy of Romania in Dhaka)
- [x] Apply via the official Romanian E-Visa portal (`eviza.mae.ro`).
- [x] **Ministry Acceptance Letter (Original)**.
- [x] **Bank Solvency Certificate**: Showing sufficient subsistence funds (minimum balance covering living costs for the first year, typically €2,500 – €3,500 equivalent in BDT).
- [x] **Official Accommodation Proof**: University dormitory contract or registered private lease in Romania.
- [x] **Comprehensive Travel Medical Insurance**: Valid for 90+ days covering minimum €30,000 across EU/Schengen.
- [x] **Valid Passport**: Minimum 18 months validity remaining.
- [x] Biometric interview and submission at the Embassy of Romania in Dhaka (Plot 4, Road 119, Gulshan-2, Dhaka 1212).
